A Letter For You

Hi, my love,

I don't really know where to begin, because no words seem enough for what I feel. But if there's one thing I want you to know, it's this: I'm grateful for you.

Thank you for coming into my life at a time when I didn't even know I needed someone like you. You didn't just become someone I loved. You became someone who made ordinary days feel a little more meaningful.

Sometimes I wonder what my life would look like if our paths had never crossed. Maybe everything would still be fine. Maybe I'd still be smiling, laughing, and living my days the same way.

But I know one thing for sure— it wouldn't be the same.

Because meeting you gave me memories I never knew I would treasure this much. Your presence became one of those little things I learned to be thankful for every single day.

I'm grateful for your patience. I'm grateful for your understanding. I'm grateful for every conversation, every laugh, every little moment, and even the quiet moments where we didn't need many words to understand each other.

I know I'm not perfect. I know there are times when I don't know how to express what I'm feeling properly. There are things I wish I could say better, things I wish I could explain without making them complicated.

But behind all my imperfections is someone who is genuinely thankful that you exist.

You are one of the most beautiful chapters life has ever written for me. And if someday you ever question your worth, I hope you remember that there is someone who is endlessly grateful that the universe allowed your story to meet mine.

I don't promise that every day will be perfect. But I promise that I will always appreciate the fact that I got to know you, love you, and call you mine.

If I could go back to the day before I met you, I wouldn't ask for a different life. I would simply wish for the same moment to happen again— because meeting you is one of the things I would never want to change.

So thank you. Thank you for being here. Thank you for choosing to stay. Thank you for becoming a part of my story.

And most of all, thank you for giving my heart another reason to believe that something beautiful can actually last.

I love you, always. ♡
— Yours
